Tripsy

Co-founder & Developer
2018 — Present

My cofounder and I have been building Tripsy since 2018, helping people plan and organize their travels. It brings itineraries, reservations, and travel details together, making it easier to plan a trip and share it with others.

Visit Tripsy or download the app.

Recognition from Apple

2024 iPhone App of the Year finalist

Apple named Tripsy an iPhone App of the Year finalist at the 2024 App Store Awards, recognizing its role in helping travelers stay organized.

In September 2026, Apple included Tripsy in its introduction of Siri AI, describing adding a restaurant recommendation to Tripsy as an upcoming app action.
